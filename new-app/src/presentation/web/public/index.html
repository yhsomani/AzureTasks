WORKSPACE CONFIGURATION / 10
Establish a trusted connection.
Workspace settings, access credentials, and maintenance in one controlled space.

        

        

        

          

            Connect your workspace safely
            
1. Configure your organization and timezone. 2. Add a least-privilege token. 3. Test access before planning tasks. Preview changes before any creation.

          

          
        

        

          Credentials are sensitive configuration
          
Never paste credentials into logs, screenshots, or a repository. Restrict access to the server's environment and configuration files. Use HTTPS and rotate any exposed token. Masked display does not mean secrets are encrypted at rest.
